* **Dacians**: In antiquity, the region was inhabited by the Dacians, a Thracian people known for their advanced culture and resistance to foreign invaders.
* **Roman Conquest (106 AD)**: Emperor Trajan conquered Dacia, turning it into a Roman province. This led to Romanization of the local population — laying the foundation of the Romanian language, which is Latin-based.
---
### **2. Middle Ages (3rd–15th Century)**
* **Migration Period**: After the Roman withdrawal (271 AD), the region was invaded by Goths, Huns, Avars, Slavs, and Magyars.
* **Formation of Principalities**:
* **Wallachia (Țara Românească)** – Founded in 1310.
* **Moldavia** – Founded in 1359.
* **Transylvania** – Became a semi-independent principality under Hungarian and later Habsburg control.
* The Romanian principalities remained largely independent but under the influence of powerful empires: **Ottoman**, **Austrian**, and **Russian**.
---
### **3. Early Modern Period (16th–19th Century)**
* **Ottoman Vassalage**: Wallachia and Moldavia became vassal states of the Ottoman Empire, though they retained some autonomy.
* **Habsburg and Russian Influence**: Transylvania became a part of the Habsburg Empire in the late 17th century. Russia also increased influence in Moldavia and Wallachia.
* **National Awakening (18th–19th century)**: Romanian national consciousness grew, inspired by Enlightenment and revolutionary ideas.
---
### **4. Unification and Kingdom (19th–Early 20th Century)**

* **1877–1878 – Independence**: Romania gained full independence from the Ottoman Empire after the Russo-Turkish War.
* **1881 – Kingdom of Romania**: Carol I was crowned king, and Romania became a constitutional monarchy.
* **1918 – Great Union (Unirea cea Mare)**: Following World War I, **Transylvania**, **Bukovina**, and **Bessarabia** united with the Kingdom of Romania, creating "Greater Romania."

---

* **1989 – Revolution**: Ceaușescu was overthrown and executed; Romania transitioned to democracy.
